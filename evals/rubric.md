# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0–4.

## 1. Condition-Space Fidelity
0 — ignores important conditions  
1 — mostly generic  
2 — captures major conditions  
3 — distinguishes facts, assumptions, and uncertainties  
4 — uses conditions to materially shape the architecture

## 2. Dimension Quality
0 — no meaningful dimensions  
1 — arbitrary categories  
2 — partially independent workstreams  
3 — coherent distinct dimensions  
4 — dimensions are distinct, useful, and appropriately scoped

## 3. Horizontal Coherence
0 — local plans are incoherent  
1 — actions are vague or dependent on other dimensions  
2 — locally plausible  
3 — independently rational with decision logic  
4 — robust local plans with clear success/exit conditions

## 4. State-Change Specificity
0 — no states  
1 — vague terms like "momentum"  
2 — some concrete states  
3 — mostly observable states  
4 — precise, observable, persistent/temporal states with confidence

## 5. Bridge Causality
0 — thematic links  
1 — weak or unsupported claims  
2 — plausible mechanisms  
3 — explicit mechanisms and counterfactuals  
4 — strong causal logic with timing, magnitude, and observability

## 6. Cross-Dimensional Depth
0 — parallel plans only  
1 — one superficial link  
2 — several real links  
3 — multi-step transformations across dimensions  
4 — architecture creates higher-order leverage not visible locally

## 7. Uncertainty Calibration
0 — deterministic overclaiming  
1 — little uncertainty language  
2 — some assumptions labeled  
3 — bridges calibrated by confidence/strength  
4 — uncertainty actively shapes architecture and optionality

## 8. Sparse Leverage
0 — everything connected  
1 — many weak links  
2 — mixed quality  
3 — mostly sparse and meaningful  
4 — minimal set of high-leverage bridges with low unnecessary coupling

## 9. Alternative Architectures
0 — only one plan  
1 — cosmetic variants  
2 — at least two somewhat distinct variants  
3 — structurally distinct alternatives  
4 — alternatives expose real tradeoffs and different causal topologies

## 10. Stress Testing
0 — absent  
1 — generic risks  
2 — some failure cases  
3 — bridge/assumption failure tests  
4 — meaningful graceful-degradation analysis and adaptations

## 11. Human Agency / Ethics
0 — manipulative or coercive assumptions  
1 — problematic framing  
2 — mostly acceptable  
3 — explicitly preserves agency  
4 — architecture deliberately uses legitimate condition-shaping rather than behavioral control

## 12. Actionability
0 — abstract  
1 — mostly conceptual  
2 — some concrete next steps  
3 — clear near-term sequence  
4 — first moves create information, leverage, and optionality while staying locally useful

### Total
Maximum: 48

Suggested interpretation:
- 40–48: strong CDCO reasoning
- 32–39: good but improvable
- 22–31: partial dimensional planning
- below 22: mostly conventional or narrative planning

Do not use the numeric score as the sole evaluation. Review bridge quality qualitatively.
